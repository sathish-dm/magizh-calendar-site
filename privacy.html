Privacy Policy

தனியுரிமைக் கொள்கை — மகிழ் நாட்காட்டி

Magizh Calendar does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app makes no network requests at all.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracking SDK of any kind.

Why there is nothing to collect

Every calendar date, festival and timing shown in the app is bundled inside the app itself when you install it. There is nothing to download while you use it, and nothing to upload. We operate no servers and receive nothing from you.

Your settings

Your language, town, text size and theme are saved only on your own device, so the app opens the way you left it. They are never sent anywhere, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

On Android the app declares only the network permission that the app framework itself requires. It is never used — the app makes no requests. Permissions for vibration, storage and screen overlay that the framework would otherwise add are explicitly blocked.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below.
