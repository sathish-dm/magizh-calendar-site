Support

The app shows no advertisements at all — is that right?

Yes. There are no advertisements, no paid upgrade, and no account. The app also makes no network requests whatsoever, which is what makes that a fact about how it is built rather than a promise.

Does it work without internet?

Completely. Every date is stored inside the app when you install it. You can keep the phone in flight mode permanently and nothing changes.

The text is too small, or too large

Open அமைப்பு / Settings from the top right and choose சிறியது, இயல்பு or பெரியது. The app also follows your phone's own text size setting, so both work together.

The timings look wrong for my town

Sunrise, sunset and the kaalam timings are calculated for the town chosen in Settings, which defaults to Chennai. Pick your own town from the list and the timings will follow.

A date does not match my almanac

The dates are computed astronomically, which follows the Thirukanitha tradition. Families who follow the Vakya (Pambu) panchangam will see a different date on some days, and both are correct within their own tradition. If you believe a date is wrong within Thirukanitha, please write — the calendar dates for 2026 have been checked against published Tamil calendars, and reports of errors in later years are genuinely useful.

How far ahead does it go?

Through the end of 2035.
